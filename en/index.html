01 — THE IDEA

Inspired by a goddess of the arts,
and the joy of music.

Uzmera takes its name from Ame-no-Uzume, a Japanese goddess of the arts. Inspired by her joyful dance and music, we brought recording and practice tools together to make time with music a little brighter.
