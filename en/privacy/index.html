Privacy Policy

Mika Spark Studio ("we", "us") explains below how information is handled in Uzmera (the "app"), our music practice app.

Effective November 17, 2026

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to Uzmera V1.0. The operator is Mika Spark Studio and the responsible person is Miyashita Kazuki. Contact information is provided below.

2. Performance and practice data

The app accesses audio from your microphone, with your permission, for features such as the tuner and recording. Recordings, practice records, settings and consumable records are stored on your device. V1.0 has no feature that automatically uploads audio or records to our servers.

When you export information using system sharing or other means, the recipient's handling of that information also applies. If you use device or operating system backups, the data may also be included in those backups.

3. Sheet music and photos

To read sheet music, the app loads the image you select and analyzes it on your device. Imported sheet music and analysis results may be stored in the app. V1.0 does not send sheet music images to our servers for analysis.

4. Product searches and network communication

When you search for or retrieve consumable product information, requests including your search criteria are sent to the product information service. The service and network providers may receive information necessary for communication, such as your IP address, access time, and device or browser information.

5. Advertising and consent management

The free plan uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show ads. Depending on the conditions for using sheet music recognition, the app may show rewarded ads. Please see the in-app information for how ads are handled with Plus.

Google and its partners may process information such as IP address, device information, identifiers and ad interactions for ad delivery, frequency management, measurement and fraud prevention. We request non-personalized ads. Google User Messaging Platform is used to collect and manage consent where required by region. Where ad privacy options are required, you can change your choices in the app settings.

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information for advertising

6. Usage analytics

We may use Firebase Analytics to improve quality. When analytics is configured and you consent in the app, information such as feature usage is sent for analysis. You can change your consent in the app settings. The app is designed not to send recording contents, sheet music images or the text of notes as analytics events. Please also see Google's policy for Firebase's handling of information.

7. In-app purchases

Plus purchases and restoration are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not directly receive payment information such as credit card numbers. To check purchase status, the app receives transaction and entitlement information from Apple and processes it on your device. See Apple's Privacy Policy for Apple's handling of information.

8. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Depending on the data, it may remain in operating system backups or similar backups. For retention and deletion of information handled by outside providers such as Google and Apple, please refer to their guidance.

9. Changing permissions and settings

You can change microphone and other permissions in your device settings. Analytics consent and, where required, ad privacy options can be changed in the app settings. If you do not grant a permission, the corresponding feature may not be available.

10. Sharing and security

We do not provide user information to third parties except as needed to use the services described above, when you choose to share it, when required by law, or in similar circumstances. We limit information handling to what is needed for its purpose and work to protect it appropriately.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or information handling change. We will publish the updated policy on this page.
